How to use: Use one row per hand hygiene opportunity. Tick every moment (M1–M5) that applies at the same time; two moments happening together count as one opportunity (one row) [HH-02]. Then tick ONE outcome in that row: Hand rub (~20–30 s), Handwash with soap and water (~40–60 s), or Missed [HH-04, HH-13]. Wearing gloves does not replace hand hygiene [HH-11].
