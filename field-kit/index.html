IPC Starter Kit – Printable Field Kit Index

All sheets below work without internet and print cleanly in black-and-white on both A4 and US Letter paper (Ctrl+P or Cmd+P). Use them alongside ipc-kit.html or as a standalone paper kit if a device cannot run the browser app.

Android / LineageOS note: If tapping links here fails with ERR_FILE_NOT_FOUND (Android content:// file openers allow only one file at a time), either open ipc-kit.html (which includes all 8 printable sheets & guides built inside the app) or open ALL-PRINTABLE-SHEETS.html directly from your Files app.

Privacy Rule across all sheets: Do not write names, ID numbers or clinical details on this sheet. Always use a ROLE (for example, Ward in-charge or IPC focal person), never a person's name [HH-10, PR-02, PR-03].
Sheet One-line description Print hint
0. All 8 Printable Sheets Combined
ALL-PRINTABLE-SHEETS.html		 Single self-contained file containing all 8 sheets below with same-page navigation (recommended if Android blocks links between files). Print full kit packet in one job (~10 pages total) or select page ranges.
1. Quick Start Guide
quick-start.html		 One-page overview of what the kit is, privacy rules, how to open the app, the 5-minute practice run, and everyday routines. Print 1 copy per care area and post near the computer or IPC binder (1 page).
2. Hand Hygiene Observation Sheet
hand-hygiene-observation-sheet.html		 One-page tally form with 20 opportunity rows, moments M1–M5, outcome checkboxes, and session totals for app entry [HH-01..HH-08]. Print 1 sheet per 20-minute observation session (fits on 1 page).
3. Stock Card & Physical Count Sheet
stock-card-and-count-sheet.html		 Page 1 tracks receipts, issues, counts, and adjustments for a single IPC supply item; Page 2 records monthly shelf counts [ST-01..ST-10]. Print 2-sided or as 2 separate pages; keep 1 Page-1 card per supply item in the store.
4. IPC Area Readiness Checklist
readiness-checklist.html		 One-page monthly walkthrough checking water, soap/handwashing, hand rub, cleaning supplies, and waste/sharps safety [RC-01..RC-06]. Print 1 copy per clinical area each month (fits on 1 page).
5. Monthly Review Routine & Action Log
review-routine-and-action-log.html		 Step-by-step agenda for a 30-minute no-blame monthly review plus a corrective action log assigned by role and due date [HH-12, RC-06, IPC-01]. Print 1 copy per monthly review meeting and file in the IPC binder (1 page).
6. Hand Hygiene Practice Scenarios
practice-scenarios.html		 Group-training worksheet with 6 hand hygiene and data interpretation scenarios on Page 1 and the trainer answer key on Page 2 [HH-09]. Print Page 1 for trainees and Page 2 for the trainer (2 pages).
7. Facility Data-Handling Plan
data-handling-plan.html		 Fill-in stewardship form covering approved devices, fixed folder path, backups, PIN lock, retention, and small-group review [PR-01..PR-03]. Print 1 copy for the facility, complete before real use, and keep signed in the IPC binder (1 page).
8. IPC Lead Review & Pilot Checklist
ipc-lead-review-and-pilot-checklist.html		 Part A pre-launch review (including mandatory source citation verification) and Part B 2–4 week pilot checklist with Go/No-Go criteria [IPC-02]. Print 1 copy before launching the kit in your facility (1 page).
