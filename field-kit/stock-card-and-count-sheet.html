Stock card rules: Balance counts usable stock only (stockout = 0 usable stock) [ST-02, ST-03]. Expired stock never counts as usable and must be removed; an expiry date is usable up to and including that day [ST-10]. Issue the earliest-expiring usable batch first (Use first) [ST-07].

Physical count rules [ST-02, ST-04, ST-05, ST-10]:
  1. Count only what you can see: Open boxes if needed and count using the item's locked unit [ST-01].
  2. Separate expired or damaged stock: Any batch past its expiry date (or damaged) is NOT usable stock. Move it out of the usable storage area immediately and record an adjustment (Expired – removed or Damaged) [ST-02, ST-05, ST-10].
  3. Enter counts the same day: Update the stock card or enter the count in Stock > Record > Physical count on the same day so balances match actual usable stock on the shelf [ST-04].
