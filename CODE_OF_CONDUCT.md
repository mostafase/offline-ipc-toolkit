# Code of conduct

This project welcomes healthcare workers, translators, and technical contributors with different levels of experience. Explain acronyms when they matter, ask questions respectfully, and critique the work without targeting the person.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted contact, and sharing someone else's private information are unacceptable. Keep patient and staff identifiers, credentials, and real facility records out of project discussions, attachments, and examples.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, and other spaces maintained by the project. For a routine moderation concern, raise it without repeating sensitive material or naming people unnecessarily. For harassment or a sensitive concern, use GitHub's reporting controls on the relevant content or account. The maintainer may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation as appropriate.
