# Disclaimer

## Intended use

The IPC Starter Kit supports staff education, aggregate hand hygiene observation, and supply inventory monitoring. It is intended for facility-level infection prevention and control (IPC) quality improvement.

This toolkit is not a certified medical device and should not replace professional clinical judgment. It is not intended for diagnosis, treatment, patient-specific recommendations, or mandatory surveillance or regulatory reporting. Follow applicable public health guidance, facility policies, and product labels. Verify stock and expiry information against physical inventory and labels before acting on a report. Observed hand hygiene results describe the opportunities sampled.

## Privacy and deployment

Use aggregate counts, facility-approved area codes, and supply records only. Keep protected health information (PHI), patient and staff identifiers, individual clinical details, and incident narratives out of all app fields, paper forms, backups, and reports. Use fictional examples in public issues or support requests.

The toolkit makes no claim of HIPAA certification or compliance. Browser storage, backups, and exports can contain confidential operational information. Keep them on approved devices and in approved storage. Review reports for possible identification through small groups or linkage with other information before sharing. The app's small-sample alert concerns interpretation of the metric and does not establish that a report is safe to share.

Have the local IPC lead review the workflow, source guidance, and any translations, and test backup and restore on the intended device before using facility data. Follow applicable local privacy and healthcare requirements.
