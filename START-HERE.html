IPC Starter Kit (v1.0.0)

Offline hand hygiene observation & IPC supply stock monitoring for clinics and hospitals.

1. Open the Offline Tool or Print the Field Kit

Designed for airplane-mode use on a supported device, with no accounts or installation. Check the device guide and test saving and restoring before entering facility data. Includes all 6 official UN languages in the app (English, العربية RTL, 中文, Français, Русский, Español — review non-English translations with a fluent local clinical reviewer before patient care use).

For IPC education and quality improvement. The toolkit is not a certified medical device and should not replace professional clinical judgment. Keep patient and staff identifiers and clinical details out of the kit. See Disclaimer.

First step: Unzip the release archive into one fixed folder before opening (never open HTML inside a .zip preview). On macOS, prefer Chrome, Firefox, or Edge rather than Safari for reliable local storage. Exported CSV files open directly in Excel (UTF-8 BOM included). If your phone or tablet cannot save local browser data, use the printable paper sheets below.

On Android / LineageOS: Do not tap links if your Files app opened this page as a content:// preview (that triggers ERR_FILE_NOT_FOUND). Open ipc-kit.html directly in Chrome/Firefox (all 8 printable sheets, Evidence Register & Device Guide are built right inside ipc-kit.html, zero external links needed), or open field-kit/ALL-PRINTABLE-SHEETS.html. See docs/DEVICE-TESTING.md.

2. Complete a 5-Minute Practice Run

  1. Click Open IPC Starter Kit App above and choose Try a 5-minute practice run.
  2. Answer the 6 fictional practice questions (overlapping moments, missed hand hygiene with gloves, and interpreting a small sample).
  3. Tally 3 hand hygiene opportunities (including one where two moments happen together) and finish the session.
  4. Record one stock issue and check the stockout, reorder point, and expiry alerts.
  5. Download a versioned backup file.

3. All Included Files

  • ipc-kit.html — Self-contained single-file offline web application (now also bundles all printable sheets, Evidence Register & Guides built-in).
  • field-kit/ — Printable implementation kit:
    • All 8 printable sheets combined in one file (works over Android content:// with same-page links)
    • One-page quick start
    • Hand hygiene observation sheet
    • Stock card and physical count sheet
    • IPC & WASH readiness checklist
    • Monthly review routine & corrective action log
    • Printable practice scenarios & answer key
    • Facility data-handling & privacy plan
    • Local IPC lead review & pilot checklist
  • evidence/evidence-register.html (CSV) — Offline evidence register separating published guidance from facility and kit design choices.
  • docs/DEVICE-TESTING.md & docs/TRANSLATING.md — Device opening/testing guide and translation guide.
  • sample-data/ — Fictional sample backup and aggregate CSV reports.
  • tools/ipc_tool.py — Optional Python 3 (standard library only) desktop helper to verify backups and combine CSV reports.
